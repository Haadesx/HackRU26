*DATABASE FIELDS — REQUIRED vs OPTIONAL*

✅ = Required
⚠️ = Conditional (required when applicable)
➖ = Optional

All timestamps are stored in UTC. Local clock times (target_time, local_time) are stored as time without timezone and interpreted in users.timezone.

*1. users*
✅ id, name, email, password_hash, role, timezone, sms_enabled (default true), push_enabled (default true), created_at
➖ phone (E.164)
Allowed roles: patient, caregiver.
Enforce: email must be unique.
Note: timezone is an IANA name (e.g. America/New_York).
Note: SMS is only sent when phone is set and sms_enabled is true.

*2. caregiver_links*
✅ id, patient_id, caregiver_id, status, permissions (default {"view": true, "receive_alerts": true}), created_at
➖ invite_code, invite_expires_at
Allowed statuses: pending, active, revoked.
Allowed permission keys: view, receive_alerts.
Enforce: patient_id + caregiver_id must be unique.
Note: caregivers are view-only. They cannot create, edit, confirm, or skip anything.

*3. medications*
✅ id, name, created_at, strength (when specified in prescription)
⚠️ openfda_id, rxcui (once matched against OpenFDA)
➖ dosage_form, verified (default false), verified_at
Enforce: lower(name) + strength + dosage_form must be unique.

*4. prescriptions*
✅ id, patient_id, version, status, created_at, effective_at (when known)
➖ source_document_uri
Allowed statuses: draft, active, superseded.
Enforce: patient_id + version must be unique.
Enforce: at most one active prescription per patient.
Note: label scans create a draft. Instructions and other label details are filled in and reviewed while finalizing the draft, before activation.

*5. prescription_items*
✅ id, prescription_id, medication_id, dose_quantity, dose_unit, route (when applicable), quantity_prescribed (for inventory tracking), refills_authorized (when specified), instructions
Note: dose_quantity = amount per dose; quantity_prescribed = total supply.
Note: dose_quantity, dose_unit, and instructions are nullable in the DB because drafts are incomplete by design; they are required at activation (checked by Flask).
Note: on activation, if stock is unknown and quantity_prescribed is set, medication_inventory is initialized from it (manual_adjustment transaction). Opt out with initialize_inventory = false.

*6. dose_windows*
✅ id, patient_id, name, kind, target_time, on_time_before_min, on_time_after_min, missed_after_min, created_at, updated_at
Allowed kinds: breakfast, lunch, dinner, bedtime, generic, custom.
Enforce: one row per patient for each of breakfast, lunch, dinner, bedtime, generic.
Enforce: all minute fields ≥ 0; missed_after_min ≥ on_time_after_min.
Note: user-editable. Edits apply only to dose events generated after the edit.

Defaults seeded when a patient registers:

| kind | target_time | on time | missed after |
|---|---|---|---|
| breakfast | 08:00 | 30 min before to 60 min after | 120 min after |
| lunch | 12:30 | 30 min before to 60 min after | 120 min after |
| dinner | 19:00 | 30 min before to 60 min after | 120 min after |
| bedtime | 21:30 | 30 min before to 60 min after | 120 min after |
| generic | set per schedule time | 30 min before to 30 min after | 120 min after |

*7. dose_schedules*
✅ id, prescription_item_id, frequency, start_date, timezone, active
➖ end_date
Allowed frequencies: daily, every_other_day, weekly (weekly repeats on start_date's weekday).
Enforce: valid schedule and at least one dose_schedule_times row.
Enforce: one schedule per prescription item (prescription_item_id unique); end_date ≥ start_date.
Note: active is false while the prescription is a draft and true once it is activated.

*8. dose_schedule_times*
✅ id, schedule_id, local_time, dose_window_id
Enforce: schedule_id + local_time must be unique.
Note: when the window kind is not generic or custom, local_time defaults to the window's target_time.

*9. dose_events*
✅ id, patient_id, prescription_item_id, medication_id, schedule_id, schedule_time_id, scheduled_at, on_time_start, on_time_end, missed_after, confirm_deadline, status, dose_mismatch (default false), confirmed_after_missed (default false)
⚠️ confirmation_method, confirmed_at, taken_at, quantity_taken (when status is taken_on_time or taken_late)
➖ observation_event_id, window_name
Note: patient_id, prescription_item_id, and medication_id are denormalized from the schedule so queries and alerts don't need a 4-table join, and so a dose taken under a superseded prescription isn't regenerated by the new version.
Allowed statuses: pending, taken_on_time, taken_late, missed, skipped.
Allowed confirmation methods: scan, manual.
Enforce: schedule_id + scheduled_at must be unique.
Enforce: on_time_start ≤ scheduled_at ≤ on_time_end ≤ missed_after ≤ confirm_deadline.
Note: window bounds are snapshotted from dose_windows when the event is generated.
Note: confirm_deadline = the next scheduled dose for the same schedule, capped at 24 hours after scheduled_at, and never earlier than missed_after (for doses scheduled close together). A missed dose can still be confirmed until then; it becomes taken_late with confirmed_after_missed = true.
Note: dose_mismatch is set when quantity_taken ≠ the item's dose_quantity.
Note: observation_event_id points at vision_observations.event_id. There is no FK because that table is a hypertable.
Note: regular table, not a hypertable, since it is updated and referenced by other tables.

*10. dose_outcomes (TigerData time-series table)*
✅ scheduled_at, dose_event_id, patient_id, medication_id, status, dose_mismatch, recorded_at
⚠️ delay_minutes (when status is taken_on_time or taken_late)
Allowed statuses: taken_on_time, taken_late, missed, skipped.
Enforce: hypertable partitioned on scheduled_at.
Enforce: dose_event_id + scheduled_at must be unique. Upsert on this key, so a late confirmation after a miss replaces the missed row.
Note: written whenever a dose reaches or changes final status. Feeds the daily_adherence continuous aggregate.
Note: dose_event_id has no FK (kept simple on the hypertable); it always comes from dose_events.

daily_adherence (continuous aggregate over dose_outcomes, real-time, refreshed hourly): per patient, medication, and UTC day: counts of taken_on_time, taken_late, missed, skipped, dose_mismatch, and total. The daily_adherence_rates view adds on_time_rate.
Note: the /adherence API buckets dose_outcomes in the patient's own timezone instead (time_bucket with a timezone), because UTC days split US evening doses into the wrong day.

*11. vision_observations (TigerData time-series table)*
✅ observed_at, event_id, patient_id, drug_name, quantity, payload (default {}), received_at
➖ strength, medication_id, dose_event_id, match_status, match_score
Note: payload holds the raw request and the match result (candidates, next dose) so idempotent replays return the same answer.
Allowed match statuses: matched, ambiguous, no_match, not_due.
Enforce: hypertable partitioned on observed_at.
Enforce: primary key is event_id + observed_at.
Note: observed_at is the scan timestamp from the client (UTC). event_id is a client-generated UUID used for idempotency.
Note: patient_id comes from the authenticated user; the user_id in the request must match it.

*12. reminders*
✅ id, patient_id, recipient_user_id, kind, title, body, scheduled_at, status
➖ dose_event_id, refill_request_id, sent_at
Allowed kinds: dose_due, dose_followup, missed_dose_alert, missed_dose_resolved, dose_mismatch_alert, refill_update.
Allowed statuses: pending, sent, failed, cancelled.
Enforce: dose_event_id + kind + recipient_user_id must be unique when dose_event_id is set.
Note: missed_dose_alert, missed_dose_resolved, and dose_mismatch_alert go to caregivers with receive_alerts. dose_mismatch_alert also goes to the patient.
Note: the patient also gets a missed_dose_alert, delivered in-app only.

*13. notification_deliveries*
✅ id, reminder_id, channel, status, attempts (default 0), created_at
➖ push_token_id, sent_at, read_at, error
Allowed channels: push, sms, in_app.
Allowed statuses: pending, sent, failed.
Note: in-app notifications are deliveries with channel = in_app; read_at applies only to them.
Note: one push delivery per push token.

*14. push_tokens*
✅ id, user_id, token, platform, created_at
➖ last_used_at
Allowed platforms: web.
Enforce: token must be unique.
Note: iOS push requires a paid Apple Developer account, which the team does not have. iOS users get in-app and SMS. Add ios to allowed platforms if that changes.

*15. medication_inventory*
✅ id, patient_id, medication_id, quantity_unit, updated_at
⚠️ quantity_remaining (once initial supply is known)
➖ last_reconciled_at
Enforce: patient_id + medication_id must be unique.
Important: unknown quantity ≠ zero. Track inventory only when initialized.
Note: inventory is tied to the medication, not a prescription version, so it carries over when a prescription is edited.
Note: low-stock thresholds are handled in the front end.

*16. refill_requests*
✅ id, patient_id, prescription_item_id, status, created_at
⚠️ requested_by (if tracking who initiated the request)
⚠️ fulfilled_at, quantity_added (when status is fulfilled)
➖ appointment_at, pharmacy_or_provider, caregiver_notified_at
Allowed statuses: requested, scheduled, fulfilled, cancelled.
Caregiver recipient can be resolved through caregiver_links.
On fulfillment, write an inventory_transactions row (reason = refill) with delta = quantity_added.

*17. inventory_transactions*
✅ id, patient_id, medication_id, delta, reason, created_at
⚠️ dose_event_id (when reason is dose), refill_request_id (when reason is refill)
➖ created_by, note
Allowed reasons: dose, refill, manual_adjustment.
Enforce: delta is negative for dose, positive for refill; non-zero otherwise.
Enforce: patient_id + medication_id must reference an existing medication_inventory row.
Note: every change to medication_inventory.quantity_remaining must have a matching transaction.

*DATABASE ENFORCEMENT*
- Primary keys and foreign keys must be valid.
- Required fields must not be NULL.
- Quantities must be non-negative where applicable.
- Status fields should have allowed values enforced.
- Duplicate scheduled doses must be prevented.
- Duplicate incoming vision events must be handled idempotently.
- Caregiver permissions must be checked by Flask. Caregivers are read-only.
- A scan links an observation to a dose event. Only the patient's tap to confirm changes dose status.
- A dose quantity that differs from the prescribed amount is flagged and triggers a dose_mismatch_alert.
- Prescription OCR edits should be reviewed before activation.
- Medications are verified against OpenFDA when entering a prescription; store the match on the medications row.
- Prescription activation should flag any medication that could not be verified against OpenFDA.
